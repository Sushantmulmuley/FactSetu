## 2. What is the lock-in for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?

Here's the lock-in position for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver: **3 years from the date of allotment of each instalment**.  
A lock-in is a period during which you can't redeem or switch your units; ELSS funds carry a statutory 3-year lock-in for each instalment, while other equity funds usually have none.  
This comes straight from the HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Scheme Information Document (SID).

## 5. Exit load of HDFC Large Cap Fund?

For HDFC Large Cap Fund, the exit load is **1.00% if redeemed/switched out within 1 year from allotment date; NIL after 1 year**.  
An exit load is a fee charged on the amount you redeem or switch out before a set period, and for an SIP each instalment's period counts from its own allotment date.  
This comes straight from the HDFC Large Cap Fund Direct Plan page on hdfcfund.com.

## 6. How do I download my capital gains statement?

You can get a capital-gains statement for HDFC Mutual Fund investments in three ways: (1) through HDFC Mutual Fund's own website using your PAN/registered email, (2) through your RTA — CAMS or KFintech — using your PAN and registered email, or (3) through the platform/distributor you invested via. Statements are usually available after the financial year closes.
